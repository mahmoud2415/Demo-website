# Project Brief — Mahmoud Restaurant

## Purpose

Build a production-like demo for presenting a complete restaurant ordering experience to prospective clients. All menu, offers, images, and branding are fictional, but the architecture and flows must be reusable for a real restaurant.

## Brand Direction

- **Brand name:** Mahmoud
- **Category:** Youth-focused burger restaurant, comparable in audience to Maestro Burger.
- **Visual direction:** Clean white base, brick-red accent, bold food-forward typography, and original assets. The structure may be inspired by Maestro's ordering experience, but Mahmoud must not copy its brand identity or content.
- **Primary language:** Arabic with RTL support. English is a future enhancement.

## Customer Experience

Visitors can browse offers, categories, best sellers, and product details; add products and notes to a cart; then submit a structured order through WhatsApp. The demo supports Delivery, Pickup, and Dine-in; each uses the same cart-to-WhatsApp flow. The public restaurant phone is `01020805451`.

## Content

The initial fictional menu will include burgers, fries, appetizers, drinks, shakes, and extras. Products will have names, Arabic descriptions, prices, images, availability, featured status, and optional add-ons.

## Administration

An authorized restaurant administrator can manage products, categories, prices, images, availability, featured products, and promotions from a dashboard. Social-media links are intentionally deferred.
